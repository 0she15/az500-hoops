No Broke Luxury Rules

The principles that protect the plan

01

No Financing Furniture

If you can't pay cash, you can't have it yet. Debt on depreciating assets destroys momentum. Save first, buy second — always.

02

One Major Purchase at a Time

Focus spending power. Trying to buy the rug, lamp, and coffee table simultaneously spreads resources thin and delays all three.

03

November Goal Always Comes First

The $4,700 November fund is non-negotiable. Every other purchase decision filters through this priority. Period.

04

72-Hour Rule on $150+ Purchases

Wait three full days before buying anything over $150. Most impulse purchases feel less urgent after 72 hours of reflection.

05

Rug & Lighting Before Decor

Foundation pieces transform a room. A beautiful rug and thoughtful lighting do more than a dozen accent pieces. Invest in the fundamentals first.

06

Maintain Emergency Buffer

Never drop below a minimum emergency reserve. The apartment plan only works if life's surprises don't derail it. The buffer is sacred.
